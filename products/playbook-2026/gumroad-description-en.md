## Design and review your interfaces

Joy N.’s UX/UI Playbook follows interface design from defining a journey to preparing error states and developer handoff. It is for developers, freelancers and teams making UX/UI decisions while implementing a product.

The English book contains **171 pages, 120 sections and nine parts**. Annotated mockups explain content, layout and behaviour choices. The 24 comparison pairs cover 13 before/after themes and 11 platform cases.

## Inside the book

- Roman numerals **I to CXX** in section running heads. The contents and cross-references also use identifiers such as 09b, L3 and A5.
- **Part 08, the component glossary**: 22 sections and **56 entries** for web, phone and dashboard. Names, definitions and contexts, with mockups and example instructions.
- Section X, reference 09b, **“What the numbers actually say”**, with dated references beneath the figures and in appendix A5, “Sources and evidence levels”.

The section’s three references:

- Nielsen Norman Group, Therese Fessenden, [“Scrolling and Attention”](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/), **15 April 2018**: desktop attention, not a measurement of reading on your page.
- Baymard Institute, [“Cart Abandonment Rate”](https://baymard.com/blog/cart-abandonment-rate), updated **28 October 2026**: an average of 50 studies published from 2006 to 2025, not your product’s rate.
- Christine Perfetti, UIE / Center Centre, [“5-Second Tests: Measuring Your Site’s Content Pages”](https://articles.centercentre.com/five_second_test/), **11 September 2007**: a method for a focused content page, less suitable for pages serving several tasks.

An average indicates where to investigate, not what to conclude for your product. No conversion gain is promised.
