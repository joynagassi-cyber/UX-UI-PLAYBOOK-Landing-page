## Description

Looking for a palette, a typeface, an icon family or a component starting point—but collecting more browser tabs than decisions?

This directory brings together **51 entries in English**, organized into **6 categories**, to help you compare options and keep a record of your choices.

### What you get

- **A 34-page A4 PDF guide**, with 51 entries and clickable links.
- **The Markdown edition** of the editorial guide, readable in a text or Markdown editor.
- **A 3-page A4 decision worksheet** to print, plus an editable Markdown version. The PDF is not an interactive form.
- **A CSV selection grid**, with 6 category rows to complete in a spreadsheet.
- A README and license notices, delivered in **one ZIP containing 7 files**.

### The six categories

1. Colors and themes.
2. Typography.
3. Icons.
4. Components and kits.
5. Data charts.
6. Illustrations and 3D.

### Three perspectives on every entry

- **Non-designer:** what to compare in a real interface.
- **Developer:** what to examine before integration and maintenance.
- **With an AI agent:** what context to provide and what to check in its proposal.

Every entry also includes **a caveat to check**. A tool alone does not guarantee a polished result, an accessible interface or error-free code.

### How to begin

Choose one screen, shortlist no more than two options per category, check rights and constraints, then complete the decision worksheet. Keeping the resources you already use is also a valid decision.

### What is free—and what may not be

**This guide is free.** The websites, services, fonts and packs it references have their own prices and licenses. Some entries group several websites: **75 links does not mean 75 distinct tools**.

No third-party font files, libraries, icon collections, illustrations or 3D scenes are included in this download. Their authors’ licenses still apply. The guide flags particular conditions for ApexCharts, unDraw and Storyset; the 75 destinations have not undergone an exhaustive availability or license audit.
